# CDV-Framework
Co-creation of Democratic Value: a framework for evaluating digital public participation platforms

## What is this?

CDV evaluates how digital platforms distribute power in participatory processes.

It measures who controls the consequential choices of a participatory process — who designs the process, who sets the agenda, who produces the content, who governs the platform, who interprets the data, who evaluates the outcome — and two conditions that run through all of them: who actually gets inside, and whether the process is trustworthy and durable. Everything is scored on documented evidence, not stated intentions.

**Five process dimensions**
- **D1 Co-Design** — who designs the process?
- **D2 Co-Agenda Setting** — who sets the topics?
- **D3a Co-Production / D3b Co-Governance** — who creates the content, and who runs the platform?
- **D4 Co-Analysis** — who interprets the data?
- **D5 Co-Evaluation** — who assesses the impact?

**Two transversal dimensions**
- **D6 Inclusion** — are marginalised groups substantively included?
- **D7 Integrity** — transparency, accountability, data protection, sustainability

Each is scored 1–5. `Total CDV = (Process × 0.6) + (Values × 0.4)`

## Contributing

A framework that demands co-creation has to be co-created. Scored cases, corrections to the rubrics and disagreements with the reference scores are all welcome — open an issue or a pull request. Every proposed change to a rubric needs cited evidence, and revisions are versioned and documented.
