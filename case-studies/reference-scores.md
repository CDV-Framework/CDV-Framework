D7 is the mean of four sub-dimensions. Barcelona: transparency 5, accountability 3.5, data protection 4, sustainability 4.5. Greece: transparency 2.5, accountability 2, data protection 3, sustainability 2.

## Reading the scores

CDV produces a profile, not a verdict. The shape matters more than the total.

Barcelona's lowest dimension is Inclusion at 2.5: the platform's weakest point is who actually gets inside. Greece's highest values are Integrity at 2.4 and Co-Production at 2.0, its lowest are agenda setting and platform governance, both at 1.0 — what works is what can be legislated and formally audited, plus the volume of text citizens produce.

Both cases were scored by a single rater. Multi-rater validation is an open task; see the repository issues, or write to the address in the README.
